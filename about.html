About Fit Zone

A neighborhood gym on Faisal St with one simple promise: this is your zone — and it never closes.

Our Story

Born On Faisal St

Fit Zone started with a frustration every lifter in Giza knows: gyms that close exactly when you finally have time to train. Between work, family, traffic and life, the old gym schedule just doesn't fit how people in this neighborhood actually live.

So we built the opposite — a gym on Talatini St that's open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. A gym where a night-shift worker trains at 3 AM, where women get a dedicated private window every day, and where a real coaching team walks the floor instead of watching from a desk.

The name says it all. This isn't our zone that you're allowed to visit. It's ur zone — and the door is always open.
