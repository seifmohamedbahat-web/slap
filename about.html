Raul Lopez

Raul Lopez founded Keeps It Real Estate to bring an institutional level of precision to San Diego's luxury housing market — without losing the personal touch that makes a home purchase feel like more than a transaction.

Over more than a decade in San Diego real estate, Raul has represented buyers and sellers across La Jolla, Del Mar, Rancho Santa Fe, Coronado, and the surrounding coastal communities, closing over $240M in cumulative sales. His approach blends sharp negotiation, meticulous market data, and a genuine investment in every client's outcome — which is exactly why 4.9 out of 5 stars across 45 reviews isn't an accident.
