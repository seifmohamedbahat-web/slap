FAQ

Quick Answers

Are you really open 24/7?

Yes — 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including holidays. Entry is secure and supervised at all hours, so 3 AM sessions are completely normal here.

Is there a women-only schedule?

Yes. Every day there's a dedicated 6-hour window reserved exclusively for women — the full gym, in complete privacy. Call 0115 425 1716 or WhatsApp us for this week's exact times.

Do you offer day passes?

Message us on WhatsApp — the team will sort you out with a visit or trial option, and you can tour the gym for free any day, any hour.

Can I pay for my membership online?

Yes — Fit Zone supports online membership payment. WhatsApp us and we'll send you the payment link to join or renew without visiting.

Do you offer personal training and nutrition guidance?

Yes. Our coaching team runs 1-on-1 personal training with goal-based programs, and can guide you on nutrition basics as part of your plan. Ask any coach on the floor.

Can I freeze my membership?

Freeze options are available on longer plans (like Annual). Ask the team about the freeze policy for your plan before you sign up.
