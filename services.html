Our Services

Everything under one roof on Faisal St — open every hour of every day. Scroll through what's inside your zone.

01 — The Core Offer

24/7 Open Access

This is what Fit Zone is built on: the gym never closes. Morning person, night owl, shift worker, exam season — train at the hour that fits your life, not the gym's schedule.

  • Open 24 hours, 7 days a week, 365 days a year
  • Mixed access around the clock (outside the ladies-only window)
  • Safe, lit and supervised entry at all hours
Ask About Access
02 — For The Ladies

Ladies-Only Hours

Every day, a dedicated 6-hour window is reserved exclusively for women. The full floor, the full equipment — in complete privacy and comfort.

  • Private 6-hour daily window, women only
  • Set up and staffed for comfort and privacy
  • Call or WhatsApp for this week's exact times
Ask About Times
03 — The Iron

Strength & Free Weights

A serious free-weights zone for people who lift: racks, bars, dumbbells and plate-loaded machines. Whether it's your first squat or your heaviest pull, the space is built for it.

  • Full dumbbell range, racks and benches
  • Machines for every muscle group
  • Coaches on the floor for form and spotting
Start Lifting
04 — The Team

Personal Coaching

Fit Zone runs on its coaching team. Get a customized program, 1-on-1 sessions and real follow-up — goal-based plans for strength, fat loss, muscle or performance.

  • Custom programs built around your goal
  • 1-on-1 sessions with a dedicated coach
  • Weekly check-ins and program adjustments
Get A Coach
05 — The Difference

Recovery Services

Most gyms stop at the last rep. Fit Zone doesn't. Post-training recovery support — stretching, massage and recovery tools — so you train more often and hurt less. Ask the team for the current recovery menu.

  • Post-workout stretching and mobility support
  • Massage and recovery tools
  • Guidance on rest, soreness and injury prevention
Ask About Recovery
06 — The Engine

Cardio Zone

Treadmills, bikes and conditioning equipment for warm-ups, fat loss and engine work — with space to move, any hour of the day or night.

  • Treadmills and bikes ready around the clock
  • Conditioning tools for HIIT and circuits
  • Coach-built cardio plans on request
Ask About Cardio

Community Events

Fit Zone regularly runs events for its members — challenges, competitions and community days. It's the reason the zone feels like a crew, not a room full of strangers.

  • Announced first on @fitzo.ne100
